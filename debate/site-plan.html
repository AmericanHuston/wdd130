Content

Home page

Community site, Type 1, Main I need a site for my community to come to. For the last few weeks, we’ve just been a free-floating un-organized group. We aren’t very effective, and when I send people to a site, it’s my general news site, not a specific site for the group. That will be the purpose of the site, alongside being able to send out events, actions, and general information for the group. I’d also like to be able to send random speech and debate students to the site to let them know about our group. Above also defines who the site is for.

The audience, Type 1 This site is for all speech and debate students, but some areas will also be specifically for my group. From now on, I’ll refer to the group as the AM or the Arcanum Messores, meaning (roughly) the Mysterious Reapers. I want people to know the purpose of the AM when they visit the site, and to want to join it after viewing the home page.

The necessity, Type 1 Furthermore, their site only posts official things, so even if I wanted to provide information via their website, I couldn’t. Perfectly reasonable, as they are technically an official business and shouldn’t tie their name with random groups that are made by teenagers.

Events

Events, Type 2, Main The second page will be about an upcoming event, and the third page will be for the history of the AM. I would frankly, really like to add a registration area to the site, but due to the restrictions of this class, I don’t know if that is fully feasible in the amount of time that is given for this assignment, and because it can’t all be run on github. I’d need to run it on my private server, which wouldn’t be on 24/7. I also thought of adding a communication area for registered users/members of the AM, but because all that wouldn’t be possible to complete with my current restrictions, I had to scrap that idea. I also have too many projects as is, so adding another to end up leaving abandoned probably isn’t the best choice.

Special information, Type 2 The information I’ll provide on this site will be some of the most unique information on the internet. The only true "competition" would be the official speech and debate website/weekly email. While their site is fantastic and works well (usually, occasionally it lags), it does not provide information for the members of the AM.

Images for the Page 2

Join Us

Pointing to external resources, Type 3, Main There are a few additional things that my site will provide, like resources to some of my other repositories that will allow the AM to use some tools I’ve made. I think that by providing these tools, I can get my message out further with the AM. No other site on the internet, other than the other news site that I made, provides this kind of information and elaboration on what tools exactly I’ve made. I’d also like to make it so that the news site points to this personal debate website, and vice versa.

Accessibility, Type 3 I want to make sure that the layout is mobile accessible, as the main way that I’ll spread the site is via QR codes at speech and debate tournaments. It’s also how I shared the news website, but for the first few months of it being online, it didn’t have a mobile friendly version. It wasn’t very reactive, so people weren’t as inclined to look at it. All of this also reminds me that I need to go back and fix some things in that project, but I honestly don’t think I will. It went way further than I ever planned, and I’m willing to let it go.
